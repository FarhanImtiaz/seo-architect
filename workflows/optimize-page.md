# Optimize a page

Inspect source/rendered evidence available for intent, metadata, headings, semantics, internal links, images, schema, CTA, direct answers, entity clarity, and evidence. Rank changes by impact. Make safe edits, validate, then report before/after and remaining review items.

Per [references/winning-patterns.md](../references/winning-patterns.md) P03, a missing title/description is a safe auto-fix, but changing the *wording/format* of an already-present title is an experiment (P01) — propose a template-level test, don't silently rewrite it.
