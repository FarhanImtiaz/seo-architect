# Internal links

Create a page graph; identify orphans, missing contextual routes to important pages, weak anchors, hubs, and stale migration links. Record proposed links in `.claude/seo/internal-links.md` with source, target, anchor, reason, priority. Prefer contextual links; avoid link stuffing.

[references/winning-patterns.md](../references/winning-patterns.md) P08 (contextual links to under-linked commercially important pages) and P05 (pillar↔cluster links) are the documented basis for prioritizing which links to add first.
