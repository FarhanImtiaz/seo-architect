# Ecommerce

Inspect categories/products, variants/facets, canonicalization, pagination, out-of-stock behavior, metadata, content duplication, image semantics, breadcrumbs, price/availability consistency, and Product schema. Do not index facets by default or assert dynamic product facts without source evidence.

[references/winning-patterns.md](../references/winning-patterns.md) P04 (Product schema needs at least one of offers/review/aggregateRating for eligibility, and every asserted value must be visible on the page) and P09 (a generated product/category page needs real per-page-unique data, or it's a doorway page) apply directly here.
